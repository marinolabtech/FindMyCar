Find My Car – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-18  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-25

This Privacy Policy applies to the Find My Car app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Marino Lab (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is provided "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

Precise Location

The Application collects the precise location of the user's mobile device. This information is used exclusively to:

  • Save the current parking position of the vehicle;
  • Provide real-time distance and direction via the built-in compass;
  • Enable navigation via Google Maps.

Location data is processed locally on the device and is never transmitted to the Service Provider's servers or to any third party for marketing purposes.

Motion Data (Activity Recognition)

If the user enables the Autoparking feature with motion detection, the Application uses the Google Activity Transitions API to detect physical activity transitions (e.g. in vehicle, still, walking). This data is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to the Service Provider.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application may automatically collect certain technical information, including device type, unique device identifier, IP address, and operating system. This is primarily handled by Google Play Services for technical stability and distribution purposes.

2. Data Stored Locally

The Application stores the following data locally in its private storage (Android sandbox):

  • The last 20 saved parking locations, each including GPS coordinates, address, date, time, GPS accuracy, and any personal notes;
  • Photos associated with parking saves, if added by the user (Premium feature). Photos are stored exclusively in the Application's private sandbox and are not accessible to other apps.

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by clearing the Application's data via Android settings.

3. In-App Purchases and Free Trial

The Application offers Premium features available for purchase via Google Play Billing. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. The Service Provider does not collect or store any payment card or payment method data. For details on how payment data is handled, please refer to the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Free Trial Period

The Application may offer a 7-day free trial for Premium features, managed via Google Play Billing. During the trial period:

  • The user has full access to all Premium features;
  • No charge is applied;
  • Google Play sends a notification before the trial expires;
  • Upon expiry, if not cancelled, the trial automatically converts to a paid purchase under the terms shown on the Application's Google Play listing;
  • The trial can be cancelled at any time via Google Play settings.

The trial and any associated payment data are managed entirely by Google Play. The Service Provider has no access to the user's payment information.

4. Location Sharing

The location sharing feature (Premium) generates a Google Maps link from the saved GPS coordinates and makes it available via the standard Android share sheet. The Service Provider does not transmit or record any data during this process. The destination of the shared link depends solely on the app chosen by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Maps / Google Earth (navigation)
  • Google Activity Recognition API (motion detection, only if enabled)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases and trial management)

6. External Links

The Application contains a link to the "Marinolab Store" (hosted on Lemon Squeezy), used for self-promotion. That external site is subject to its own privacy policy.

7. Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Security

Locally stored data is protected by the standard Android application security sandbox. The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect user information, though no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be communicated by updating this page with the new version and its effective date. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
